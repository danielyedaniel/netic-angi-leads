A lead finds its tenant by the tenant's own webhook URL, by the crmKey Angi echoes back, or by the Angi account id in the payload. A lead from an account that is not mapped here is quarantined, and is processed as soon as the mapping is added. If a tenant's own URL or crmKey and an existing mapping disagree about whose lead it is, the lead is quarantined too. Correcting the mapping here releases it. Correcting the URL registered with Angi only helps the leads that follow: the one that is waiting has to be assigned to the mapped tenant through the admin API, or discarded.
